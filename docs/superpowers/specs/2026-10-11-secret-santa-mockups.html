Secret Santa, inside Telegram

Round three: three themes (Light, Dark and Christmas), a wishlist and gift preferences, and a way to reach your recipient. These are mockups, not the app.

What you told me

  • Anyone can create an exchange, gets a code, and anyone with the code joins.
  • The creator starts the draw; everyone then sees their recipient's nickname and address.
  • newEach person can add a wishlist link and gift preferences, both optional, or choose "Surprise me".
  • newLight and dark themes, plus Christmas as its own separate theme. Each person picks their own; Auto follows Telegram.
  • newBudget has an optional minimum and maximum; the creator sets the currency. The creator can edit name, budget and deadline until the draw, and move the deadline after it.
  • newSanta and giftee can message each other anonymously, both ways, through the bot. This replaces the profile link.
  • newThe creator sets a date gifts should arrive by. Each person marks "I received my gift"; when everyone has, the exchange closes.
  • newSantas mark their gift as sent, with an optional tracking number the giftee can see.
  • newAfter the deadline, the creator can nudge the Santas whose giftees are still waiting, without learning who they are.
  • newExchanges close when all gifts arrive, or 30 days after the deadline. If a gift never arrived, the giftee gets a kind apology, the creator is told, and the creator sees who that Santa was, can message them directly, and can block them from the bot. Addresses and chats are deleted at closing.
  • newMinimum 3 people. App and bot messages in English and Russian.
  • guessWhen names are drawn, the bot messages everyone with a button that opens their recipient.

What I'm guessing

  • guessPeople can still edit their wishlist after the draw, since it only helps their Santa.
  • guessThe creator takes part too, joining closes at the draw, and nobody can draw themselves.
